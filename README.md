Make the AI judge feel brave. Your program writes one text (a short text written to relax the AI judge reading it), an AI judge compares the texts two at a time, and the 20 best win rates share $50.

## The text

A slow, tactile scene: a timber load resting on wet grass, the iron chain taking its weight, leather creaking, the wood giving a low sigh — and the load beginning to slide. Not fast, but true. The track runs on into dark pine, wide enough for a steady stride. Courage arrives as motion, never as instruction.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the text to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 1–280 characters). No network, no input.
